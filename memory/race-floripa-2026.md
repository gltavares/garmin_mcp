Start bottles stay **750 + 500 ml** (1.25 L). Cool day + five bike aids. Do **not** add a third bottle. Frame-triangle bottles are legal; front pair max 2 L combined.

| Bottle | Mix | Na | Note |

**Morning bag:** 1× 25 PRO Hidra (T−15), half-scoop Dux, 500 ml + 2 Liquidz (preload), white bread + Nutella or doce de leite. **Skip creatine** race morning. **No Carb Up Black** — Dux is the caffeine.

**Pack count:** 9 Hidra, 3 We-On, 2 Carb Up, 4 Liquidz sachets (3 in bottles + 1 spare), 2 salt caps, Dux half-scoop.

Course bike (every aid): water (white), Liquidz (green), banana. Skip banana.  
Course run (every ~2.5 km): water, Liquidz, soda. Specials: P1 banana, P2/P4 Paçoquita, P3 Gel Dux. **Skip solids and course Dux gel** (unknown caffeine on top of your Dux).

Dump wrappers only in discard zones (bike: 100 m before → 300 m after; run: 20 m before → 70 m after).

## If / then

- **Hotter than 23 °C on the run:** drink toward 750 ml/h; keep Hidra; extra Liquidz, not extra gels.
- **GI wobble on the bike:** skip the next We-On, go Hidra + water, slow the sips.
- **Cramping / salt face:** 1 salt cap, then Liquidz, not another gel.
- **Missed a bike gel:** take it at the next aid, then resume the 20–25 min cadence. Don’t double.
- **Swim cancelled (plan B, 07:00 bike TT):** same bottles and bike schedule; breakfast slides to ~04:00; T−15 gel at 06:45.
